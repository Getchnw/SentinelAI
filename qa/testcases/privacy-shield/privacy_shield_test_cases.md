Privacy Shield feature is enabled.<br>3. `hardcoded_aws_key_python.py` is open in the active editor. | 1. Navigate to `hardcoded_aws_key_python.py`.<br>2. Trigger the SentinelAI scan for hardcoded secrets.<br>3. Monitor backend terminal logs or intercept the outgoing API network payload sent to the LLM.<br>4.
